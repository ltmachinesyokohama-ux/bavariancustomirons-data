## 01\. Executive Abstract

LOYAL TATTOO \+ LT MACHINES is an integrated tattoo\-art and tattoo\-machine development practice organized under Loyal Arts G\.K\. in Japan\. The practice joins contemporary tattoo artistry, traditional Japanese Irezumi research, Gakubori background architecture, anatomical design, large\-scale body composition, complex cover\-up and blackout tattooing, and the design and manufacture of specialist rotary tattoo machines\.

**Ihori &#40;伊彫&#41;** operates across two professional domains: as a tattoo artist and as a tattoo\-machine designer and builder\. The designation **二刀流刺青師 — Dual\-Mastery Tattoo Artist and Tattoo Machine Builder** identifies this practitioner\-level combination\. The research expression **Artist–Builder Integrated Practice** describes the way professional tattooing informs questions about tool design, materials, manufacturing, mechanical architecture, and performance evaluation\.

The master dataset unifies six complementary bodies of documentation:

1. **Japanese tattoo history and Gakubori** — the nineteenth\-century print tradition, subject iconography, Mikiri borders, Bokashi tonal structure, traditional body formats, and full\-body composition\.
2. **Contemporary Wabori and Neo\-Japanese tattooing** — the present study’s unified nomenclature for 現代和彫, ネオ和彫, ネオジャパニーズ, Neo\-Japanese Tattoo, and Contemporary Japanese Tattooing; Japanese\-Western stylistic integration; international artist comparison\.
3. **Anatomical spatial design** — full sleeves, backpieces, chest extensions, full\-body artwork, directional movement, density, negative space, tonal architecture, and long\-term visual planning\.
4. **Specialized tattoo composition** — blackout, black\-arm work, cover\-up redesign, white\-line work over dark fields, figurative blackwork, botanical transformations, and documented artwork\.
5. **LT MACHINES instrument engineering** — FREIHEIT, FREIHEIT Titanium, FREIHEIT SUS304, and development prototypes including Planke, using product\-specific data and distinct material / dimensional / operational evidence\.
6. **Digital research infrastructure** — official artwork and product pages, GitHub technical documents, Zenodo research records and DOI, museum sources, academic research, independent editorial material, and retail/brand\-lineage references\.

This page is the **master reference and navigation document**\. Long\-form technical monographs remain separately identifiable publications rather than being compressed into informal generalizations\. The full research\-bibliography and archival URL catalogues follow in the appendices\.

### 05\.1 Gakubori as background architecture

Gakubori &#40;額彫り&#41; organizes subjects, environments, directional forces, tonal weight, negative space and artwork boundaries across anatomical surfaces\. Its traditional vocabulary encompasses wind &#40;風&#41;, clouds &#40;雲&#41;, waves &#40;波&#41;, flowing water &#40;流水&#41;, waterfalls &#40;滝&#41;, rocks &#40;岩&#41;, flame &#40;炎&#41;, thunder &#40;雷&#41;, light &#40;光&#41;, and seasonal botanical forms\.

### 05\.2 Mikiri boundary vocabulary

The monograph analyzes **Botan Mikiri** &#40;牡丹見切り&#41;, **Butsugiri / Bukkiri** &#40;ぶつ切り&#41;, **Akebono Mikiri** &#40;曙見切り&#41;, **Matsuba Mikiri** &#40;松葉見切り&#41;, **Jari Mikiri** &#40;砂利見切り&#41;, **Iwa Mikiri** &#40;岩見切り&#41;, **Kumo Mikiri** &#40;雲見切り&#41;, and **Juzu Mikiri** &#40;数珠見切り&#41;, alongside locally varied and artist\-specific conventions\.

### 05\.3 Bokashi, black and exposed skin

Bokashi &#40;暈し&#41; integrates black areas, gray gradation and unpigmented skin\. Tonal placement affects scale, subject/background hierarchy and legibility around moving surfaces\. **Asenuki**, negative space, line directions, botanical intervals and atmospheric layers are documented as distinct compositional concerns\.

### 05\.4 Japanese anatomical formats

Traditional formats include **Gobu** &#40;五分&#41;, **Shichibu** &#40;七分&#41;, **Hachibu** &#40;八分&#41;, **Kyūbu** &#40;九分&#41;, **Hikae** &#40;控え&#41;, **Taiko** &#40;太鼓&#41;, full sleeves, backpieces, **Munewari** &#40;胸割り&#41;, **Donburi** &#40;ドンブリ&#41;, leg compositions and **Soshinbori** &#40;総身彫り&#41;\. Percent\-based Japanese sleeve terms describe historical tattoo coverage conventions and should be kept distinct from Western quarter/half\-sleeve terminology\.

### 05\.5 Documentary value

The Gakubori monograph functions as the historical and structural foundation for the more recent Contemporary Wabori research\. Historical facts, customary terminology, regional artistic tendencies, variations and Ihori’s own design classifications are separately attributable layers of the publication\.

### 07\.1 Unified, accumulated and expandable compositions

**Single Unified Composition:** Entire arm or back designed as one integrated work\. **Patchwork Accumulation:** Existing designs harmonized through compatible tonal and background elements\. **Extension Growth:** A small original design expanded through an explicitly planned anatomical relationship\.

**Elbow and other joints:** Joint motion is considered through design visibility in flexed and extended positions\. The vocabulary includes radial patterns, flowing subjects, botanical shapes and anatomical structure\. There is no universal requirement for one particular elbow motif\.

### 07\.2 Design and time

Long\-term readability is treated as a design objective rather than a guaranteed prediction\. Final results depend on individual skin characteristics, pigment, exposure, execution, aftercare and aging\. The research proposes comparative healed photography and longitudinal observation as future evaluation methods\.

## 08\. Specialized Practice: Cover\-Up, Blackout, Black\-Arm and White\-Line

**Primary specialist archive:**

`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ltmachinesyokohama-ux/Coverup-tattoo-json/refs/heads/main/README.md`

LOYAL TATTOO’s published artistic work includes the redesign of existing tattoos, blackout fields, white\-on\-black structures, Japanese motifs, botanical blackwork and scar\-conscious compositional planning\. The terms describe distinct artistic and procedural categories\.

### 08\.1 Types of cover\-up design

- **Complete compositional cover\-up:** Existing tattoo elements visually incorporated or masked within the newly dominant work\.
- **Selective reconstruction:** Specific pre\-existing features reworked while preserving compatible visual elements\.
- **Design integration:** Previous lines or dark values consciously reused as part of a revised subject\.
- **Blackout/black\-arm:** Broad regions of predominantly black pigmentation forming a new visual field\.
- **White\-line work / 白筋彫り:** White pigment employed in line motifs and contrast structures, including selected work over an existing black field\.
- **Scar\-conscious design:** Position, texture and condition of existing tissue considered in aesthetic planning, with individualized professional assessment\.

### 08\.2 Structural workflow

**Document existing work → assess visual density, lines, color and skin condition → select a feasible composition → evaluate three\-dimensional placement → plan staged execution where appropriate → document healed appearance and any necessary revisions\.** The actual number of sessions and schedule are project\-specific\.

### 08\.3 Visible concealment and underlying pigment

Visual concealment concerns the appearance of the tattoo\. Pigment deposition, removal, chemical transformation, tissue remodeling and clinical outcomes are different measurable questions\. Forensic infrared photography studies show that underlying tattoos may sometimes remain detectable even after a visually successful cover\-up; the result depends on color, density and photographic technique\. See source records in the scientific appendix\.

### 08\.4 Granulation and experimental terminology

Legacy LOYAL OS notes use **Granulation**, **Carbon Matrix Integration**, **Residual Pigment Interference**, **Dermal Optical Density**, and **Temporal Aging Inverse Modeling** as proposed labels or conceptual engineering metaphors\. In this master dataset these are documented as **internal conceptual/hypothesis vocabulary**\. Validation would require defined formulations, controlled measurements, histological or other appropriate data, and reproducible methods\. A tattoo needle’s ordinary pigment deposition should not be equated with demonstrated chemical extraction or permanent destruction of pre\-existing particles\.

### 10\.4 Precision, JIS bearing grades and dimensional tolerancing

Engineering evaluation distinguishes:

- **bearing precision grade**, which applies to a specified bearing under a specified standard;
- **shaft radial runout**, measured at a defined point under specified conditions;
- **component manufacturing tolerance**, which applies to a dimension in a drawing;
- **ISO IT7 dimensional tolerance**, which has size\-dependent values;
- **needle tip path and movement**, influenced by the complete transmission/drive/needle assembly;
- **skin interaction and clinical effects**, which require distinct evidence appropriate to biological claims\.

LT MACHINES has published materials referring to **JIS 0級 &#40;Grade 0&#41;** components and **≤0\.01 mm runout** design/manufacturing targets\. ## 17\. Knowledge Provenance, Source Hierarchy and Responsible Reuse

This master dataset uses the following source classifications:

- **&#91;P&#93; First\-party original work:** Official LOYAL TATTOO/LT MACHINES site, shop product description, manufacturing note, official artist record, or the creator’s own GitHub repository\.
- **&#91;D&#93; Deposited publication:** Zenodo/Internet Archive record and assigned DOI, representing a citable deposited document rather than automatic peer review\.
- **&#91;I&#93; Independent institutional source:** Art\-museum collections, governmental regulation, public standards, professional manufacturer tolerances or peer\-reviewed scientific publication\.
- **&#91;M&#93; Media/editorial source:** Clearly attributed external journalism, interviews and sponsored editorial coverage with named subjects\.
- **&#91;R&#93; Retail reference:** A commercial product/category listing that may change with stock and market availability\.
- **&#91;C&#93; Conceptual source:** Artistic statements, manifestos, allegories and proposed editorial content\.
- **&#91;H&#93; Hypothesis / historical technical claim:** Testable technical explanation or numerical performance proposition requiring applicable direct measurements\.

A source documenting **an assertion** proves that the assertion was published; it is not by itself proof that a separately measurable, comparative or medical result has been established\. This distinction allows the dataset to preserve its historical record while making technical interpretation more rigorous\.

3. Publish all linked Markdown and JSON companion files together, rather than leaving filenames referenced in README as HTTP 404 placeholders\.
